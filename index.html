TikTok Video Downloader

Download HD and Full-HD TikTok videos without watermarks. No ads, no waiting room. Paste a link and the file downloads automatically. Batch links are processed several at a time with automatic retry.

How it works

  1. Copy a TikTok video URL from the app or your browser.
  2. Paste it into the input (or tap Paste from Clipboard).
  3. The video downloads automatically as username-timestamp.mp4.

Features

  • HD and Full-HD video, watermark-free
  • No ad gate, no waiting room
  • Batch downloads — many links at once
  • One-tap clipboard paste
  • Automatic retry on failure
  • Sensible filenames (username-timestamp.mp4)

Frequently asked questions

Are the downloaded videos watermark-free?

Yes. Videos are saved as clean HD or Full-HD MP4 files with no TikTok watermark.

Can I download multiple TikTok videos at once?

Yes. Paste as many links as you want and the app processes several at a time, retrying automatically if a download fails.

Do I need to create an account or watch ads?

No. There is no sign-up, no ad gate, and no waiting room — paste a link and the file downloads automatically.

What format are the files saved as?

MP4 video files, named username-timestamp.mp4 so they sort cleanly on your device.
